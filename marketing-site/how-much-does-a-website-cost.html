How much does
a website cost?

The short answer: in the UK in 2026, a small business website costs anything from around £10 a month on a DIY builder to £2,000 to £6,000 from an agency, plus £20 to £150 a month to host and look after it. Here is what you get at each price, so you can choose with confidence.

Updated November 2026 · By Aisha Ansari, founder of MONOS Technology

UK website prices at a glance

OptionTypical costBest for
DIY website builder£10 to £40 per monthTesting an idea, very tight budgets, people with time to design it themselves
Freelance designer£500 to £2,500Simple brochure sites where you can manage the project yourself
Small business agency site£2,000 to £6,000Businesses that need to look established, rank on Google and turn visitors into enquiries
Online shop (e-commerce)£5,000 to £15,000+Selling products online with payments, stock and delivery
Custom web app£15,000+Bookings, memberships, portals and anything bespoke

Ranges are typical UK market prices in 2026 and vary with the number of pages, features and how much content needs writing.

What changes the price of a website?

  • Number of pages. A five-page site costs less than a 30-page site.
  • Custom design or template. A custom design costs more, but it makes you look different from competitors using the same theme.
  • Features. Online booking, payments, shops, member areas and integrations all add time.
  • Content. Copywriting, photography and video are often the hidden cost. Ask whether they are included.
  • SEO set-up. A site with no SEO groundwork can look great and still never be found.
  • Speed. Faster turnaround needs a team that can commit the time.

Ongoing costs to budget for

  • Domain name: about £10 to £20 a year for a .co.uk or .com.
  • Hosting: £5 to £50 a month depending on speed, security and support.
  • Maintenance: £50 to £300 a month for updates, backups and small changes.
  • Marketing: optional, for SEO, social media or ads once the site is live.

A cheap website is worth it if it brings in more than it costs.
